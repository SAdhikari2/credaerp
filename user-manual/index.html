1. Getting Started: First 7 Days Guide

Transitioning to a new ERP requires a structured approach. Follow this strict sequence to ensure your financial ledgers start correctly.

Day 1: Setup & Initial Balances

  1. Pharmacy Profile: Navigate to System Settings → Company Profile. Enter your Pharmacy Name, accurate GSTIN, and Registered Address. This data prints on your invoices and GSTR-1 files.
  2. Populate Medicine Master: Use the Bulk Import tool to load your medicines.
  3. Enter Opening Stock: Go to Inventory → Stock Entry to map the physical boxes currently sitting on your shelves.
⚠️ Critical Constraint: Opening Stock vs. Purchase

Never use the "Procurement / Purchase" screen to enter your initial existing shop stock.

Purchases strictly create Vendor Payable ledgers and GST Input Tax Credit (ITC). If you enter old stock as a new purchase, your accounting will show that you owe massive amounts to suppliers, and your GST ITC will be artificially inflated. Always use Stock Entry for initial stock.

💡 Scenario: I entered Opening Stock, but found a hidden box later

If the financial year hasn't closed and you haven't filed GST for the month, you can safely go back to Inventory → Stock Entry and add the missing batch. The system uses a specific equity ledger (OPENING_BALANCE_EQUITY) which will safely absorb the addition without touching vendor accounts.

2. Sales & Billing (POS Terminal)

The POS terminal is optimized for rapid, high-volume pharmacy billing.

Creating a Standard Sale

  1. Press F1 to open the POS Terminal.
  2. Scan the barcode, or type the first 3 letters of the medicine to trigger the search.
  3. Select the required batch from the popup. (Verify Expiry Date visually before clicking).
  4. Enter the exact quantity (See Pack Sizes rule below).
  5. Select the Payment Method (Cash/Card/UPI/Credit) and press Save.
💊 How to Enter Quantity Correctly (Strips vs Tablets)

CredaERP tracks inventory internally at the lowest base-unit (Tablet/Capsule) to prevent calculation errors. When entering quantity on the POS or Purchase screen, use one of these two methods:

  • Method A (Total Base Pieces): Type the total number of tablets. If a strip has 10 tablets and you want to sell 1 strip, type 10. If you want to sell 2 strips, type 20.
  • Method B (Strips + Loose Formula): Type using the plus sign. To sell 2 strips and 5 loose tablets, type 2+5. To sell 1 strip, type 1+0. The system mathematically translates this based on the item's configured pack size.

💡 Scenario: Split Tender Payments (Half Cash, Half UPI)

If a customer's bill is Rs. 150, and they give you a Rs. 100 note and want to GPay the remaining Rs. 50:

In the POS screen, do not just click "Cash". Use the Multi-Pay / Split Payment button. Enter `100` in the Cash box and `50` in the UPI box. This ensures your end-of-day drawer count and your bank reconciliation match perfectly.

💡 Scenario: Printer Runs Out of Paper / Power Cut During Print

If the transaction was saved but the printer fails, do not re-enter the bill! The stock has already been deducted. Simply put new paper in the printer, press F1, go to Invoice History, select the top invoice, and click Reprint.

3. Procurement & Purchases

Procurement handles inbound distributor invoices, stock increases, and GST ITC calculations.

Logging a Supplier Invoice

  1. Press F2 or navigate to Procurement → New Purchase.
  2. Select the Vendor.
  3. Input the exact Invoice Number and Date printed on the physical bill.
  4. Scan or search for the items received.
  5. Enter Batch, Expiry, and Quantity (using the Strips+Loose format).
  6. Verify that the calculated Invoice Total matches the physical paper bill before hitting Save.

💡 Scenario: Free Schemes (Buy 10, Get 2 Free)

Distributors often provide bonus quantities. Enter 10 in the Qty box and 2 in the Free Qty box.

What the system does: It adds 12 physical units to your inventory for selling, but calculates the Purchase Rate (COGS) and GST ITC solely based on the cost of the 10 paid units. This correctly dilutes your average purchase cost, increasing your reported profit margins when you sell them.

💡 Scenario: Purchase Rate is Higher than MRP?

If you accidentally type a Purchase Rate (e.g., Rs. 150) that exceeds the MRP (e.g., Rs. 120), CredaERP will highlight the row in red and prevent saving. A pharmacy cannot legally purchase medicine above the printed MRP. You must correct the typo before the system allows the invoice to commit.

4. Deep Dive: Returns, Reversals & ITC Impact

Returning stock is the most complex operation in an ERP because it simultaneously touches Inventory, General Ledger Accounting, and Government GST Compliance. CredaERP handles all of this automatically, but you must use the correct screens.

A. Sales Returns (Customer returning medicine to you)

When a patient returns a medicine, you must use the Sales Return screen (also known as a Credit Note in GST terms).

What happens under the hood when you process a Sales Return:

  • Inventory Impact: The exact quantity (e.g., 4 loose tablets) is immediately added back to the specific Batch's physical stock.
  • Accounting Impact: Your daily revenue is decreased. If they paid Cash, your cash drawer expected balance drops. If it was a Credit Sale, the customer's outstanding balance drops.
  • GST Impact: A Credit Note is generated against the original B2C/B2B invoice. This automatically reduces your Output Tax Liability for the current month's GSTR-1.

💡 Scenario: Returning medicine from a previous closed month

If a customer returns a medicine bought in March, but it is now May (and March GST is already filed), DO NOT try to delete or alter the old March invoice. The GST portal will reject it. Process a standard Sales Return today. CredaERP will date the Credit Note for May, which legally reduces your May tax liability, keeping you 100% compliant with GST laws.

B. Purchase Returns (You returning medicine to a Distributor)

When you return expired or damaged stock to a distributor, you must use the Purchase Return screen (also known as a Debit Note).

What happens under the hood when you process a Purchase Return:

  • Inventory Impact: The stock is deducted from your shelf.
  • Accounting Impact: The system decreases your Vendor Payable balance (meaning you owe the distributor less money).
  • GST Impact (ITC Reversal): Because you initially claimed Input Tax Credit (ITC) when you bought the medicine, returning it means you must give that tax credit back to the government. CredaERP automatically records an ITC Reversal via a Debit Note, which will correctly populate in your GSTR-3B under "ITC Reversed".
⚠️ Warning: Stock Adjustment vs Purchase Return

If a bottle breaks on your floor, do NOT use Purchase Return. A Purchase Return tells the government that the distributor took the bottle back and refunded you. If the bottle is just broken, use Inventory → Stock Adjustment. This writes off the cost as a business loss but does NOT attempt to reverse the GST ITC.

5. Inventory & Expiry Management

Maintaining an accurate digital ledger compared to your physical shelves is the most critical task for a pharmacy owner.

Handling Expiries

Medicines expire on the last day of the printed month (e.g., 04/2026 expires on April 30, 2026). To prevent selling expired stock, CredaERP automatically blocks the batch from the POS screen once the date is reached.

⚠️ Avoid Dead Stock Loss

Distributors only accept returns 3-6 months before expiry. Go to Reports → Expiry Report on the 1st of every month. Set the filter to "Next 90 Days", pull those physical boxes from your shelves, and process a Purchase Return to send them back to the vendor.

💡 Scenario: Same Medicine, Same Price, Different Expiry

If you receive Crocin Advance Batch A (Expires 2026) and Batch B (Expires 2027) with identical MRPs, the system tracks them independently. When billing, the POS will prompt the cashier to select a batch. Always enforce the FEFO rule (First Expiry, First Out). The cashier must look at the physical strip in their hand and select the exact matching batch in the software.

💡 Scenario: Stock Adjustment for Broken Bottles

If a syrup bottle breaks on the floor, you cannot return it to the vendor, and you cannot sell it. You must write it off.

Go to Inventory → Stock Adjustment. Select the batch, enter quantity 1, set type to Damaged, and save. This removes 1 bottle from stock and records an accounting loss to ensure your end-of-year profit report is accurate.

6. Understanding Reports (What tells you what)

The Reports module (F8) translates raw database transactions into actionable business intelligence.

Report Name What it tells you How the system calculates it
Stock Report (Valuation) The total monetary value of medicines sitting on your shelves right now. Used for insurance or bank loans. System multiplies the Current Physical Quantity of every batch by its specific Purchase Rate (Landing Cost). It does NOT use MRP for valuation.
Gross Profit Report How much money you actually made after deducting the cost of the medicines sold. (Total Sales Value without GST) minus (Purchase Rate of the exact batches sold).
Expiry Report Which medicines will turn into dead-stock soon. Scans all batches with a quantity > 0, filtering for Expiry Dates within your selected range (e.g., Next 90 Days).
Vendor Ledger Exactly how much money you owe to each distributor. (Total Purchases) minus (Purchase Returns) minus (Payments Made to Vendor).
Customer Ledger Which credit customers owe you money. (Total Credit Sales) minus (Sales Returns) minus (Payments Received).

7. Deep Dive: GST Issues & Resolutions

GST filing can be stressful. CredaERP automates the math, but you must understand how to fix data entry mistakes.

GSTR-1 vs GSTR-3B in CredaERP

  • GSTR-1 (Sales): When you export GSTR-1 from CredaERP, it contains all your Output Tax. B2C sales (walk-in patients) are grouped by tax rate. B2B sales (clinics with GSTINs) are listed invoice-by-invoice.
  • GSTR-3B (Summary): This report summarizes your Total Sales Tax minus your Input Tax Credit (purchases). This tells you exactly what you have to pay the government.

💡 Scenario: The portal is rejecting my B2C Invoice due to a "Taxable Value Mismatch"

Why this happens: In pharmacy retail, MRP includes GST. Sometimes, reverse-calculating the tax creates a 1-paisa decimal mismatch compared to the GST portal's rigid math.

How CredaERP fixes it: CredaERP uses a specialized B2C inclusive rounding hack. It forces the Taxable Value to absorb any fractional penny differences so that (Taxable + Tax) = Exact Cash Paid. You do not need to intervene; just export the JSON from the GST Filing screen and upload it.

💡 Scenario: I billed an item at 18% GST, but it should have been 12%

The Wrong Way: Changing the GST rate in the Medicine Master. This will NOT fix the old invoices that are already saved in the database.

The Right Way: You must Cancel the wrong invoice in Invoice History (if it's the same month), fix the Medicine Master, and re-bill the customer. If the month is closed, you must issue a Sales Return (Credit Note) for the 18% item, and issue a fresh invoice at 12%.

8. Migration from Another Software

If you are switching from Marg, Retailio, or another software:

  1. Export your medicine master and stock from your old software to Excel/CSV.
  2. Go to MAINTENANCE → System Settings → Bulk Import.
  3. Upload the CSV file.
  4. Important: The CSV Quantity must be the NET base-unit count (e.g., 750 tablets, not 50 strips).
  5. Click Start Import. The migration tool handles Opening Stock safely via OPENING_BALANCE_EQUITY without generating fake Vendor Payables or false GST ITC.

9. Disaster Recovery: Backup & Restore

Hardware fails. Hard drives crash. Ransomware happens. Your data is your business.

🛡️ Best Practice: The 3-2-1 Backup Rule

At the end of every day, go to System Settings → Database → Create Backup. Save the file to your computer. Then, immediately copy it to a USB Pen Drive (Local Copy) AND upload it to Google Drive (Cloud Copy).

💡 Scenario: Performing a Database Restore

If your computer crashes and you buy a new one, install CredaERP, go to Database settings, and click Restore Backup.

CRITICAL POST-RESTORE REQUIREMENT: The restore process drops and replaces every table in the PostgreSQL database in the background. Once the success message appears, the database locks are volatile. You MUST close CredaERP completely and restart it. If you attempt to process a sale immediately after clicking Restore without restarting, the application will deadlock.

10. Operational Checklists

☀️ Opening the Shop

  • Log in to CredaERP.
  • Count the physical loose cash in the drawer and ensure it matches the Opening Balance shown on the Dashboard.
  • Review the Low Stock Alert widget to see what needs to be ordered today.

🌙 Closing the Shop (EOD)

  • Go to Day Closure (EOD).
  • Count your physical cash. Compare it to the Expected Cash in the system.
  • Check your UPI/Card machine settlement totals against the system's UPI/Card totals.
  • If there is a shortage (e.g., Rs. 50 missing), investigate. If unresolved, record a Cash Shortage Expense to balance the drawer.
  • Click Close Day. This locks the day's invoices so they cannot be tampered with tomorrow.
  • Generate and save your Daily Backup.

💡 Scenario: Forgot to Close the Day?

If you rush home and forget to run Day Closure, the next morning the system will warn you that the previous day is still open. Simply run the Day Closure for the previous date before generating any new bills. The system handles date boundaries safely.

12. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I contact support?

If you encounter a database corruption error or need license assistance, click the Contact Us link in the top menu, or email credaerp@gmail.com or call/WhatsApp 8777527458. For operational questions ("How do I do X?"), please search this manual first.

Can I use CredaERP if the internet goes down?

Absolutely. CredaERP is designed as an offline-first desktop application. The PostgreSQL database runs directly on your local hard drive. You can continue billing, purchasing, and managing stock indefinitely without an internet connection. Internet is only required for software updates and generating E-Invoices (IRN) if you cross the turnover threshold.

What do I do if a customer returns medicine from 2 months ago?

First, verify your pharmacy's return policy. If you accept it, use the Sales Return screen. Since the financial month is already closed and GST filed, the system will generate a Credit Note for the current month, which will automatically adjust your current month's GST liability downward. Do not try to delete the old invoice!

A patient wants a custom discount on a specific bill, how do I apply it?

On the POS screen, beneath the item grid, there is a Bill Discount field. You can enter an absolute amount (e.g., Rs. 50 off) or a percentage (e.g., 5%). The system will automatically prorate this discount across all taxable items to ensure the GST calculations remain mathematically compliant.

  • Fixed: Pack-size decimal truncation. Inventory math now utilizes strict Big-Decimal arithmetic to prevent losing fractional tablets during conversions.
