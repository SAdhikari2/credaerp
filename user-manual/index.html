1. Getting Started: First 7 Days Guide

Day 1: Setup & Opening Stock

  1. Login: Open CredaERP and log in using your admin credentials.
  2. Pharmacy Profile: Go to System Settings → Company Profile and enter your Pharmacy Name, GSTIN, and Address.
  3. Add Medicines: Go to Inventory to add medicines manually, or use the Bulk Import tool if you have an Excel sheet from an old software.
  4. Enter Opening Stock: Go to Inventory → Stock Entry to add the physical stock you currently have in your shop.
Critical Warning: Do not use the "Purchase" screen to enter opening stock. Opening stock must be entered via "Stock Entry" or "Bulk Import" to prevent creating fake vendor payables and fake GST Input Tax Credit.

Day 2: Daily Operations

  1. Billing (POS): Press F1 to open the POS Terminal. Practice making a cash sale and printing an invoice.
  2. Procurement: Press F2 when a new distributor box arrives. Enter the Vendor, Invoice No, Batch, Expiry, and Purchase Rate.
  3. Day Closure: At the end of the day, go to Day Closure (EOD) to match your physical cash drawer with the system.

2. Sales & Billing (POS Terminal)

How to Create a New Sale

Use this when a customer is buying medicine at the counter.

  1. Press F1 or click POS Terminal.
  2. Click the search bar and type the medicine name or scan the barcode.
  3. A popup will show available batches. Select the correct batch (verify the expiry date and MRP).
  4. Enter the quantity.
Important Note on Pack Sizes (Strips & Tablets)
CredaERP expects you to enter quantity in two ways:

Method A (Total Pieces): Type the total number of tablets. If a strip has 10 tablets and you want to sell 1 strip, type 10 in the quantity box.
Method B (Strips + Loose): Use the plus sign. If you want to sell 2 strips and 5 loose tablets, type 2+5. The system will automatically calculate the total correctly.
  1. Apply any discounts if applicable.
  2. Select the Payment Method at the bottom (Cash, Card, UPI, Credit).
  3. Click Save & Print.

What happens if I make a mistake?

If you billed the wrong item or wrong batch, you can cancel the bill (if done on the same day) via Invoice History, or process a Sales Return. Never manually adjust the stock for a returned item! Processing a proper Sales Return ensures the GST and Accounting ledgers are reversed correctly.

3. Purchase & Procurement

How to Enter a Supplier Invoice

Use this when you receive new stock from a distributor with a tax invoice.

  1. Press F2 or click Procurement.
  2. Click New Purchase.
  3. Select the Vendor from the dropdown (or add a new one).
  4. Enter the Supplier's Invoice Number and Date.
  5. Search for the medicine.
  6. Enter the Batch No, Expiry, and Quantity (using the total pieces or Strips+Loose format).
  7. Verify the Purchase Rate, MRP, and GST %.
  8. Click Save Purchase.
Handling Free Schemes (e.g., Buy 10 Get 2 Free)
When making a Purchase, enter 10 in the Quantity box and 2 in the Free box. CredaERP will add 12 to your physical stock, but calculate the Purchase Rate and GST based only on the 10 paid items.

4. Inventory & Stock Management

Understanding the Pharmacy Quantity Model

CredaERP uses a dual-quantity model to prevent confusion between strips and tablets.

  • Pack Size: Set in the Medicine Master (e.g., 15 tablets per strip).
  • Total Pieces: CredaERP automatically multiplies strips by the pack size in the backend. If you buy 10 strips of 15 tablets, CredaERP correctly adds 150 tablets to your physical stock ledger.

How to Adjust Damaged or Expired Stock

If you find a broken bottle or an expired strip that cannot be returned to the vendor:

  1. Go to Inventory → Stock Adjustment.
  2. Select the medicine and batch.
  3. Select the Adjustment Type (e.g., Damaged, Expired).
  4. Enter the quantity to remove (use the total pieces or Strips+Loose format).
  5. Provide a remark and Save. This safely removes the stock and writes off the financial loss in accounting.

5. Customers & Vendors

Managing Customer Accounts

To create a customer profile (useful for chronic patients or credit sales), go to CORE SALES → Customers. You can view their purchase history and outstanding balances.

To receive a payment for a credit sale:

  1. Open the Customer profile.
  2. Click Receive Payment.
  3. Select the invoice to settle and the payment mode (Cash/UPI/Bank).

Managing Vendors

Go to OPERATIONS → Vendors to view supplier ledgers. When you pay a distributor, open their ledger and record a Vendor Payment to decrease your outstanding payable balance.

6. GST & Compliance

How does CredaERP handle GST?

CredaERP is designed for strict Indian Pharmacy GST compliance.

  • Sales (B2C): POS prices are inclusive of GST. CredaERP accurately reverse-calculates the Taxable Value, CGST, and SGST so the invoice total exactly matches the cash paid by the customer.
  • Purchases (B2B): Purchase entry calculates Input Tax Credit (ITC) based strictly on the supplier invoice.

How to Export GST Data for your CA

  1. Go to FINANCIALS → GST Filing.
  2. Select the month or quarter.
  3. Choose GSTR-1 (for sales) or GSTR-3B (for summary).
  4. Click Export JSON or Export Excel.
  5. Send this file to your Chartered Accountant or upload it directly to the GST Portal.

Disclaimer: For official tax compliance decisions, always consult your CA. CredaERP provides the operational calculations.

7. Reports & Analytics

Go to INSIGHTS & AI → Reports & Analytics (F8) to access your business data.

  • Sales Report: View total revenue, discounts given, and item-wise sales.
  • Profit Report: Calculates Gross Profit based on the actual Purchase Rate of the specific batches sold.
  • Expiry Report: Vital for pharmacy operations. Filter by "Next 30 Days" or "Next 90 Days" to pull stock before it expires.
  • Stock Valuation: Shows the total monetary value of your current inventory, useful for insurance and balance sheets.

8. Migration from Another Software

If you are switching from Marg, Retailio, or another software:

  1. Export your medicine master and stock from your old software to Excel/CSV.
  2. Go to MAINTENANCE → System Settings → Bulk Import.
  3. Upload the CSV file.
  4. Important: The CSV Quantity must be the NET base-unit count (e.g., 750 tablets, not 50 strips).
  5. Click Start Import. The migration tool handles Opening Stock safely via OPENING_BALANCE_EQUITY without generating fake Vendor Payables or false GST ITC.

9. Disaster Recovery: Backup & Restore

How to Backup your Data (Daily)

  1. Go to MAINTENANCE → System Settings → Database.
  2. Click Create Backup.
  3. A .sql or .backup file will be generated. Copy this file to a USB drive or Google Drive immediately! Do this daily.

How to Restore your Data (If your PC crashes)

  1. Install a fresh copy of CredaERP on the new PC.
  2. Go to System Settings → Database.
  3. Click Restore Backup and select your file.
CRITICAL RESTART REQUIRED: After the success message appears, you MUST close and restart CredaERP immediately. The restore engine replaces the entire database behind the scenes. If you try to bill without restarting, the application will crash.

10. Troubleshooting Decision Trees

❌ My Stock is Incorrect!

Did you check the physical shelf?
→ Yes.

Is it wrong for just one batch, or all batches?

  • Just one batch: Check if a recent Purchase was entered with the wrong quantity.
  • All batches: Check if the Pack Size (Strips vs Tabs) in the Medicine Master is wrong.

How to fix: Use Inventory → Stock Adjustment to correct the physical count.

❌ My End-of-Day Cash Doesn't Match!

Did you process a UPI or Card payment but accidentally click "Cash" on the POS screen?
→ Check the Invoice History for today. You can edit the payment method without canceling the bill.

Did you take cash out of the drawer for a shop expense (e.g., tea, stationery)?
→ You must record this in FINANCIALS → Journal Voucher as an Expense so the system knows the cash left the drawer.

11. Operational Checklists

Daily Closing Checklist

  • Reconcile Cash: Ensure the physical drawer matches the system ledger in Day Closure (EOD).
  • Run Backup: Generate a database backup to a USB drive.

Month-End Checklist

  • GST Preparation: Export GSTR-1 and send it to your CA.
  • Expiry Check: Check the Expiry Report for medicines expiring next month. Pull them from the shelf and initiate Purchase Returns to distributors.

Financial Year-End Checklist

  • Full Backup: Take a complete database backup before closing the year.
  • Year-End Wizard: Run the Financial Year End tool from the Maintenance menu. This safely carries forward stock and ledger balances to the new financial year.
  • Invoice Series: Update your invoice prefix (e.g., from SALE-2526/ to SALE-2627/) in Settings.

13. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I use CredaERP without the internet?

Yes. CredaERP is an offline-first desktop application. The PostgreSQL database runs directly on your computer. You only need the internet for software updates or E-Invoice (IRN) generation.

What happens if I accidentally close the day?

Day closure locks the previous transactions to prevent tampering. If you made a mistake, an Admin user can use the "Reopen Day" function in the Day Closure screen (this action is heavily audited).

What do I do if the application crashes during a sale?

CredaERP uses strict database transactions. If the power fails right as you click Save, the system rolls back. When you restart, either the sale never happened (stock is safe), or the sale completed fully. There are no "half-finished" bills.

Can I sell partial strips without breaking the stock?

Yes! Simply type 0+5 (or just 5) in the quantity box. The system correctly deducts exactly 5 tablets from the master stock without messing up your strip counts.
